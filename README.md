# Conflict Driven Evolution

Enemy evolution responds to how you play. Expanding your factory, polluting the
world, and killing enemies can make them grow stronger. Destroying nests and
worms helps push evolution back, while trees soften the effect of pollution.
Time still matters, and you can adjust how strongly each factor counts.

This mod is for players who want their choices in expansion and combat to shape
enemy progression. Fighting has a trade-off: killing enemy units adds pressure,
but clearing their bases can reduce it.

## Optional features

- **Technology cap:** Keeps enemy evolution in step with your research progress.
- **Session report:** Shows how each factor has changed evolution since you
  joined. The breakdown appears in Stats GUI if you have it installed, or
  periodically in chat otherwise.

Made for Factorio 2.0 and Space Age. Avoid combining it with another mod that
changes enemy evolution.
